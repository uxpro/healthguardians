What the humanoids do

Use cases

Every humanoid in our fleet offers some of four features: fall detection, conversation, sentiment analysis and reminders. We combine them into two use cases.

Use case 01

Fall detection

The humanoid stays in the room and watches for falls and stumbles. If the patient does not get up within a minute, the event is raised again as a more urgent one.

Your dashboard shows

  • Events as they happen, with an alert animation
  • Environmental data: temperature, humidity, air quality
  • Friendly reminders the humanoid gives, based on its features
  • Robot log with date, time and payload

Use case 02

Conversation support

A robotic face asks the patient how they are, whether they took their medication, and shows a short exercise. The answers become events: a worrying or missing answer becomes an alert.

Your dashboard shows

  • Each step of the daily check-in
  • Alerts: does not feel well, medication not taken, no answer
  • The robotic face, to try the conversation yourself
  • Robot log and humanoid state

Event types

Every event a humanoid sends has a code from 0 to 255. The code range sets the type.

TypeCodesExamples
Keep alive0-63Heartbeat, self test passed, reminder delivered, check-in steps
Environment variable alarm64-127Room temperature, humidity, air quality, floor obstacle
Fall detected128-191Fall, fall without movement after 60 s, stumble
Emergency192-255Emergency button, patient does not feel well, no answer, humanoid critical fault
