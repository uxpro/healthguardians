Business model

To be decided: the business model is still under discussion. This page lists the working assumptions.

Health Guardians is a humanoids-as-a-service company. Clinics rent care humanoids with the service included; HG Group owns the fleet, keeps it running and earns a recurring fee per unit.

Business model canvas

Working version

Key partners

  • Humanoid OEMs A, B, C and D
  • Field service technicians
  • Transport and logistics
  • Cloud hosting provider

Key activities

  • Fleet purchase and refurbishment
  • Deployment and on-site tests
  • Monitoring and predictive service

Value proposition

  • Falls noticed within seconds
  • Daily check-ins without extra staff time
  • No capital cost, no repair worries
  • No patient names leave the clinic

Customer relationships

  • Dedicated support desk
  • Customer dashboard
  • Quarterly service review

Customer segments

  • Clinics and hospitals
  • Care homes
  • Home-care providers (later)

Key resources

  • Humanoid fleet and warehouse
  • Event gateway and dashboards
  • Trained technicians

Channels

  • Direct sales to clinics
  • OEM partner referrals
  • Healthcare fairs

Cost structure

  • Fleet purchase or leasing from OEMs
  • Technicians, travel and spare parts
  • Warehouse, hosting and support

Revenue streams

  • Monthly rental fee per humanoid, service included
  • Rental period extensions
  • Deployment and staff training fee
  • Optional faster service levels

What we do not do

  • We do not sell humanoids or hardware.
  • We do not store patient names or medical records.
  • We do not sell or share event data.

Open questions

  • Price per humanoid and per use case
  • Minimum rental period
  • Service levels and response times
  • Which OEM models enter the fleet first
