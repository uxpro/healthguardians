Cookies and stored data

This website sets no cookies for tracking or advertising. It keeps three items in your browser's local storage, all of them needed for the site to work.

NamePurposeKept until
hg.sessionYour sign-in session (access token) for the dashboardsYou sign out, or the session expires
hg.consentRemembers that you have seen the notice about stored dataYou clear your browser data
hg.a11yYour accessibility settings: text size, contrast, motion, linksYou reset them or clear your browser data

How to remove them

Sign out to remove the session. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
