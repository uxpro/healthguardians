Frequently asked questions

Short answers about renting, privacy and the dashboards.

Renting

Can we buy the humanoids?

No. Health Guardians only rents humanoids. The rental includes maintenance, spare parts, monitoring and support.

How long is a rental period?

The rental period is agreed per humanoid. You can request an extension from the customer dashboard.

What happens when a humanoid breaks?

We see it on our side too. A technician repairs it on site or we replace it with a unit from our warehouse.

Privacy

Do you know who our patients are?

No. Each deployed humanoid is linked to an anonymous patient ID. Only your clinic can match it to a person.

What does your logistics team see?

Availability and health of the machines: status, integrity indicators, time to failure, parts and service. Not the content of events and not the patient IDs.

Are conversations recorded?

No audio is stored. Only the step of the check-in and the answer button chosen are sent as an event.

Dashboards

How fast do events reach the dashboard?

The dashboard asks the server for new events every few seconds, so a new event usually appears within three seconds.

Can we export the events?

Yes. The customer dashboard exports the events to a file that opens in Excel.

Who can sign in?

Accounts are created by HG Group for each customer. Customers see their own humanoids only.

Technology

What is an event code?

A number from 0 to 255 that tells what happened. Its range sets the type: keep alive, environment variable alarm, fall detected or emergency.

What do the three lights mean?

They are the integrity indicators: green is normal, yellow means action is needed, red is critical.
