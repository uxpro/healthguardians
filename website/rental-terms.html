Rental and service terms

Draft: this text is a working version and will be reviewed before launch.

Rental period

Each humanoid is rented for an agreed period that starts on the day it is deployed and tested at your clinic. You can ask for an extension at any time from the customer dashboard; the extension starts when HG Group confirms it.

What is included

  • Delivery, setup and on-site test
  • Scheduled maintenance and repairs
  • Spare parts and replacement units
  • Event gateway, dashboards and support

Your obligations

  • Keep the humanoid in the agreed rooms
  • Give our technicians access for service
  • Report damage through a support ticket
  • Keep the link between patient ID and person inside your clinic

End of the rental

At the end of the period we collect the humanoid, erase its local data and check it in our warehouse. Events stay available to you for export for 30 days, then they are deleted.
