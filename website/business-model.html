Company

Business model

Health Guardians for Elder Citizens is a European sovereign Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) integrator for private clinics in Germany and the Copenhagen area. Clinics rent two-arm service robots for a monthly fee with no upfront investment; we source open ROS 2 hardware, secure it with our own Sovereign Care Gateway, connect it to the building and hospital IT, and keep it running with a swap within 24 hours.

Why private clinics: no public tendering, so a sales cycle of 2–4 months instead of 12+. In Copenhagen, high Scandinavian wages and a strong welfare-technology culture make the time savings worth even more.

The problem

Skilled staff run errands

Up to 30% of a nursing shift goes to walking: lab samples, sterile goods, medication, drinks, consumables. Missing staff directly limits how many procedures and bed days a private clinic can sell.

Robots are hard to buy

A two-arm assistive robot costs €35,000–€40,000+ upfront, and lean private clinics have no mechatronics staff to run it. Cheap tray robots have no arms for doors, lifts or hand-overs.

Data leaves Europe

Mobile cameras and microphones talking to non-EU vendor clouds are rejected by data protection officers, CISOs and works councils.

The sovereign stack

4 pillars

1 · Open ROS 2 fleet

Wheeled two-arm robots from several vendors (about €25,000 landed). Wheels instead of legs: no fall risk, longer battery life.

2 · Sovereign Care Gateway

Our edge box (about €1,000 per robot): zero-trust firewall that blocks non-EU vendor clouds, faces blurred and audio scrubbed on site, encrypted tunnel to servers in Germany/EU (ISO 27001, BSI C5).

3 · EU orchestration platform

Connects wards, lab, pharmacy, lifts, doors, nurse call and the hospital information system (HL7/FHIR, VDA 5050), with anonymised facility analytics.

4 · Turnkey field service

Installation, staff training, preventive maintenance and a pre-configured swap robot from our regional hub within 24 hours.

Hardware candidates being benchmarked include AgiBot G2, Galbot G1, Galaxea R1 Pro and NEURA MiPA (EU dual source). The live demo humanoid Roha2212 runs our own HealthGuardians app on a 3D-printed head.

Go to market

Land & expand

Target clusters

  • Germany: private clinic groups and specialist clinics (orthopaedics, surgery, cardiology, rehab) in the south-west and Rhine-Main; one successful pilot opens the sister clinics
  • Copenhagen: private hospital groups and specialist clinics under strong cost pressure and short waiting-time targets

Who decides

  • Buyers: managing, commercial and medical directors (sales cycle 2–4 months)
  • Champions: directors of nursing, OR and outpatient coordination
  • Gatekeepers: IT lead / CISO, data protection officer and staff representatives (works council in Germany, MED committee in Denmark)

Compliance package

GDPR

No identifiable video or audio leaves the facility; logs hosted in Germany/EU (ISO 27001, BSI C5); DPIA support for German authorities and the Danish Datatilsynet.

EU product rules

CE conformity under the Machinery Regulation, Cyber Resilience Act (SBOM, managed security updates), AI Act transparency. Non-medical use only, so no MDR approval is needed.

Staff representatives

Process-over-person analytics: no employee IDs, no shift-roster linkage, k-anonymity, with a ready-made agreement template for works councils (DE) and MED committees (DK).
