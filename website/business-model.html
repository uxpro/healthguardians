Business model

Health Guardians for Elder Citizens is a European sovereign Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) integrator. Hospitals and care homes rent two-arm service robots for a monthly fee with no upfront investment; we source open ROS 2 hardware, secure it with our own Sovereign Care Gateway, connect it to the building and hospital IT, and keep it running with a swap within 24 hours.

The problem

Nurses run errands

Up to 30% of a nursing shift goes to fetch-and-carry: water, consumables, lab samples, non-urgent night calls. Staff shortages already close beds.

Robots are hard to buy

A two-arm assistive robot costs €35,000–€40,000+ upfront, and care facilities have no mechatronics staff to run it. Cheap tray robots have no arms for doors, lifts or hand-overs.

Data leaves Europe

Mobile cameras and microphones talking to non-EU vendor clouds are rejected by data protection officers, CISOs and works councils.

The sovereign stack

4 pillars

1 · Open ROS 2 fleet

Wheeled two-arm robots from several vendors (about €25,000 landed). Wheels instead of legs: no fall risk, longer battery life.

2 · Sovereign Care Gateway

Our edge box (about €1,000 per robot): zero-trust firewall that blocks non-EU vendor clouds, faces blurred and audio scrubbed on site, encrypted tunnel to servers in Germany/EU (ISO 27001, BSI C5).

3 · EU orchestration platform

Connects wards, lab, pharmacy, lifts, doors, nurse call and the hospital information system (HL7/FHIR, VDA 5050), with anonymised facility analytics.

4 · Turnkey field service

Installation, staff training, preventive maintenance and a pre-configured swap robot from our regional hub within 24 hours.

Hardware candidates being benchmarked include AgiBot G2, Galbot G1, Galaxea R1 Pro and NEURA MiPA (EU dual source). The live demo humanoid Roha2212 runs our own HealthGuardians app on a 3D-printed head.

Go to market

Land & expand

Karlsruhe beachhead

  • Multi-site non-profit care operators (20+ homes): one reference ward, then a group framework contract
  • Welfare associations: regional innovation and care-digitalisation grants
  • Private care operators: fastest pilot decisions
  • Hospitals in phase 2, with care-home references and full HIS integration

Who decides

  • Buyers: managing and facility directors, CFOs (sales cycle 3–6 months in care, 6–12 in hospitals)
  • Champions: directors of nursing and ward leads
  • Gatekeepers: works council, data protection officer, CISO, hygiene officer, served by our compliance package

Compliance package

GDPR

No identifiable video or audio leaves the facility; logs hosted in Germany/EU (ISO 27001, BSI C5); DPIA support.

EU product rules

CE conformity under the Machinery Regulation, Cyber Resilience Act (SBOM, managed security updates), AI Act transparency. Non-medical use only, so no MDR approval is needed.

Works council

Process-over-person analytics: no employee IDs, no shift-roster linkage, k-anonymity, with a ready-made works agreement template.
