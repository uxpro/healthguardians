How it works

Rent

Choose the use case. We pick the humanoid model that fits your rooms and patients, from four OEM partners.

Deploy

Our technicians deliver, set up and test the humanoid on site and link it to an anonymous patient ID.

Monitor

Every event goes through our gateway to your dashboard within seconds: falls, alarms, check-ins.

Maintain

We watch each unit's health, plan the service before it fails and swap parts from our own warehouse.

Use case 01

Fall detection

The humanoid watches over the room, detects falls and stumbles, reports the room climate and gives friendly reminders. Your staff see the event the moment it happens.

  • Fall and near-fall events
  • Temperature, humidity and air quality
  • Reminders based on the robot's features

Use case 02

Conversation support

A friendly robotic face runs a short daily check-in: mood, medication and a guided exercise. Worrying answers become alerts on your dashboard.

  • Daily check-in conversation
  • Alerts for worrying or missing answers
  • Guided movement exercises

Privacy by design

We know the robot, not the patient.

Each deployed humanoid is linked to an anonymous patient ID that only your clinic can match to a person. Our logistics team sees availability and health of the machines, never what happens in the room.

Read the privacy policy

Two dashboards

One for your clinic, one for our fleet.

The customer dashboard shows your humanoids, their events, the robot log and the linked patient ID. The logistics dashboard tracks every unit: where it is, its integrity indicators, estimated time to failure, spare parts and service history.
