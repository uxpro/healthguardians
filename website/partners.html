Become an OEM partner

  1. Send us your model's data sheet and safety certificates.
  2. We test the model in our lab with the event gateway: codes 0-255 and the four event types.
  3. Pilot deployment at one clinic for three months.
  4. Your model joins the fleet, with spare parts in our warehouse.

Become a pilot clinic

Pilot clinics try new models and features first, help us improve the dashboards and get better rental conditions while the pilot runs.
