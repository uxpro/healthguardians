Success stories

Two demo clinics show how Health Guardians works in practice. Their names and numbers are fictitious.

Demo customer

Central Clinic

Fall detection, 4 humanoids

Central Clinic placed four humanoids in rooms where patients are often alone at night: two of the same OEM A model, one OEM C and one OEM D. The night team now sees a fall on the dashboard within seconds instead of on the next round.

"We used to find falls on the round. Now the round finds us."
Head nurse, Central Clinic (demo)
Demo customer

North Clinic

Conversation support, 3 humanoids

North Clinic uses three OEM B humanoids with the conversational feature. Every morning the robotic face asks each patient how they feel and whether they took their medication. "Not so good" or a missed dose shows up as an alert.

"Patients talk to it more than we expected, and we hear about bad days earlier."
Ward manager, North Clinic (demo)
