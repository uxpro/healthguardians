About us

Mission, vision and values

HG Group runs Health Guardians: a fleet of care humanoids that clinics rent, with the service, spare parts and monitoring included.

Our mission

Give care teams a reliable extra pair of eyes and a friendly voice for their patients, without asking clinics to buy, repair or understand robots.

Our vision

Every patient who is alone in a room has a guardian that notices a fall within seconds and asks every day how they feel, while every patient's identity stays with their clinic.

Our values

Safety first

A missed fall costs more than any robot. We design every process around fast, correct alerts.

Privacy

No patient names, no recordings in our systems. Anonymous IDs only.

Reliability

We measure each humanoid's health and service it before it fails, not after.

Open to every OEM

We pick the best humanoid for the job from several manufacturers, not just one.

The Health Guardians platform

Humanoids

Models from four OEM partners with fall detection, conversation, sentiment analysis and reminders.

Event gateway

Every message a humanoid sends goes through our gateway: keep-alive, environment alarms, falls and emergencies, each with a code from 0 to 255.

Dashboards

Customers follow their humanoids and events; our logistics team follows availability, integrity and service.

Warehouse

Humanoids ready to deploy and spare parts for every OEM, so a broken part does not stop the care.

Field service

Technicians who install, test, maintain and repair the humanoids on site.

Support

One support desk for clinics, whatever the model or manufacturer.
